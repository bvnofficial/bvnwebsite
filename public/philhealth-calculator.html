🏥 What Your PhilHealth Covers
With an active PhilHealth membership, you and your qualified dependents are entitled to:
• Inpatient care: Room and board, professional fees, medicines, lab tests during hospital confinement
• Day surgeries & outpatient procedures: Hemodialysis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy
• Z-benefit packages: Full coverage for catastrophic illnesses (cancer, rare diseases)
• Maternity care: Normal delivery, caesarean section, prenatal care
• Free preventive care: Annual check-up package for members and dependents
