Our Story

From Cake Company to Full Kitchen

Stacy Hilliard has been baking for the Bonham community for over a decade through Juniper Cake Company. Juniper Kitchen is the next chapter — a full scratch kitchen where every dish is made the way home cooking should be: with quality ingredients and genuine care.


We opened at 110 E Russell Ave to serve Bonham families the kind of food you can feel good about. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.
