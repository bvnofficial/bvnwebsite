Section 01

Full job workflow, lead to completed install

Every job sits in exactly one status, so at any moment you can look at the board and know what is waiting on you, what is waiting on the customer, and what is waiting on money. No job hides.

01
New Enquiry
Web form, call or GHL lead lands as an unscheduled job with source tagged.
02
Site Inspection
Booked to a sales diary. Inspection form captures the home and the load.
03
Quote Sent
Quote template by system type, sent with the accept link.
04
Quote Follow Up
Automated nudges at day 2, 5 and 10 until accepted or lost.
05
Deposit Due
Accepted quote triggers the deposit request. Nothing is ordered before it clears.
06
Scheduled
Install booked, crew assigned, customer gets confirmation and a day before reminder.
07
On Site
Installer checklist and photos. Job cannot complete until required photos exist.
08
Final Payment
Invoice on completion, certificate and manuals attached, reminders until paid.
09
Warranty & Care
Review request, warranty record, service reminder scheduled ahead.
Section 02

Templates that stop people typing the same thing twice

Quote templates, job templates, message templates. Built once, chosen from a list, priced consistently by system type.

Q Quote templates

  • Ducted reverse cycle, zones, ducting run, controller, electrical allowance, crane or roof access
  • Multi split, outdoor unit sized to head count, per head line items, pipe run
  • Single split, back to back and long run variants
  • Heat pump hot water, changeover, tempering valve, rebate line and eligibility note
  • Shared blocks: inclusions, exclusions, warranty terms, deposit terms, payment terms

J Job templates

  • Sales inspection, prefilled tasks, inspection form attached, 60 minute default
  • Install day, materials list, crew allocation, checklist and photo set attached
  • Service or fault call, diagnostic form, parts used, return visit path
  • Warranty visit, links to the original install job and its serials
  • Each template pre attaches the right forms so nobody picks the wrong one

M Customer message templates

  • Enquiry received, what happens next and when we call
  • Inspection confirmed, then on my way with a live arrival window
  • Quote sent, then the three follow ups
  • Deposit received, install date confirmed, day before reminder
  • Install complete with manuals, warranty and the review link

$ Deposit and final payment

  • Deposit percentage set on quote acceptance, job blocked from Scheduled until paid
  • Ordering and crew booking only unlock after the deposit clears
  • Final invoice raised the moment the install checklist is signed off
  • Automatic reminders at day 3, 7 and 14, then it flags for a call
  • Owner dashboard shows deposits pending and money outstanding by job

Section 04

ServiceM8 connected to GoHighLevel

The two systems do different jobs. GoHighLevel owns the lead, the marketing conversation and the follow up. ServiceM8 owns the job, the crew, the paperwork and the money. The connection keeps one truth between them so nothing is entered twice and nothing goes quiet.

GoHighLevel
Lead and conversation
  • Website forms, calls, missed call text back
  • Ad and referral source tracking
  • Nurture for leads not ready yet
  • Review requests and reputation
  • Seasonal service reminder campaigns
lead → creates job
status → updates stage
quote sent → nurture on
deposit paid → nurture off
job complete → review ask
ServiceM8
Job, crew and money
  • Job card, statuses and scheduling
  • Quotes, deposits and invoices
  • Forms, checklists and photos
  • Serials, warranty and documents
  • Installer app in the field
Why this matters. I have already built and shipped a live GoHighLevel to ServiceM8 sync for an Australian trade client. It went live on 29 July and runs daily. That is the same bridge described above, not a theory.
