Section 01

Your question, answered first: what I would automate before anything else

You asked what I would automate first for a service business getting most of its leads from Instagram and the website, to turn more inquiries into paying clients. Here is the specific answer.

Speed to first reply, then a deposit link inside that same first reply. Not a chatbot. Not a nurture sequence. Those come second. The single biggest leak in a tattoo studio is that an inquiry arrives at 9pm on Instagram, gets read the next afternoon, and by then that person has already messaged three other artists. Whoever answered first is booking them.

1 Why this one first

  • It is the only stage where the lead is still deciding. Everything after booking is protecting revenue you already won. This stage creates it
  • Tattoo inquiries are impulsive and comparative. People message several studios in one sitting. Response order beats response quality more often than anyone likes to admit
  • A deposit is the real conversion event, not a reply. An inquiry that says yes and never pays is not a client. So the first automation must end in a payment link, not a conversation
  • It costs almost nothing to run and it needs no rebuild of your website, your CRM or your booking tool to start paying for itself

2 What that actually looks like

  • Every inquiry lands in one inbox. Instagram DM, story reply, comment, website form and email all pipe into the same CRM conversation view. No tab hopping
  • An instant, human sounding first message goes out within two minutes, day or night, that asks the four qualifying questions and nothing more
  • AI reads the answers and scores the lead as ready to book, needs a consult, or not a fit, then tags the record and routes it
  • Ready to book gets a booking link with the deposit attached. Slot is only held once the deposit clears
  • Needs a consult goes to you or an artist with the answers already summarised, so the human conversation starts at message five, not message one

Section 04

The website side, in order of what actually moves bookings

You asked about mobile, conversion, forms, lead capture, speed and basic SEO. Here is how I would sequence it, because doing these in the wrong order wastes weeks.

First
Make the inquiry form ask the four questions
Your form is the highest leverage object on the site. If it collects name, email and message, every lead arrives unqualified. If it collects placement, size, reference and budget band, the AI can score it and the deposit link can go out instantly. Same amount of typing for them, completely different lead.
Second
Fix the mobile path from Instagram bio to form
Nearly all of your traffic arrives on a phone, from a link in a bio, in an in app browser. I would test that exact path and cut every tap between landing and submitting. Usually there are three too many.
Third
Connect the form to the CRM with a webhook, not an email
Right now a form submission almost certainly becomes an email in an inbox. That is a lead with no timer on it. Once it posts to the CRM it enters the same two minute reply flow as the DMs.
Fourth
Page speed and the gallery
Tattoo sites are heavy because the portfolio is the sales pitch. Compress and lazy load properly and you keep the images and lose the three second wait that costs you the visit.
Fifth
Basic SEO with intent that converts
Style plus city pages, artist pages, and a real FAQ answering price, pain, healing and deposits. Local search intent for a studio is small in volume and very high in intent. Worth doing, worth doing last.
Section 05

The AI layer for you and your VAs

You said you want help making your team use AI properly, and systems documented so they are easy to follow. That is not a training session, it is a set of assets that live where the work happens.

A prompt pack, not prompt tips

Saved, named prompts for the jobs your VAs actually repeat: reply to an inquiry in studio voice, write a caption for a healed piece, turn a session into a case study post, draft the monthly email. Stored in the CRM as snippets so nobody has to remember anything.

Voice guardrails

One document that defines how the studio talks, what it never says, how it handles price questions and how it declines work. Every AI reply is generated against it. This is what stops automation sounding like automation.

SOPs written as checklists

Short, screenshotted, one job per page. Written so a new VA can run it on day one without asking you. Living in one place, versioned, not scattered across chat history.

A weekly numbers email

Inquiries by source, reply time, inquiry to deposit rate, no show rate, rebooking rate. One email, Monday morning. You cannot improve the funnel you cannot see.

A human override on everything

Every automated sequence has a stop condition and a way for a person to take the wheel mid conversation. Personal communication was your requirement, so it is a design rule, not an afterthought.

Documentation you own

Account access, credentials, scenario maps and rebuild notes handed over as we go. If I disappear tomorrow the studio still runs. That is the standard I hold myself to on every build.

Section 06

The prompt contract behind the AI scoring

Not a vibe. A contract, so the output is the same shape every single time and the CRM fields never break.

SYSTEM
You classify tattoo inquiries for one studio. You never invent details.
You never quote a final price. You only use the studio voice document.

INPUT
inquiry_text, source, reference_image_present, existing_client (true/false)

RETURN strict JSON, no prose:
{
  "placement":        string | null,
  "size_cm":          number | null,
  "style":            string | null,
  "first_tattoo":     true | false | null,
  "budget_band":      "under_min" | "at_min" | "above_min" | "unknown",
  "urgency":          "this_month" | "flexible" | "just_asking" | "unknown",
  "verdict":          "book" | "consult" | "decline",
  "confidence":       0.0 to 1.0,
  "missing":          [ fields still unanswered ],
  "summary":          one sentence for the artist
}

RULES
confidence below 0.7        -> verdict is "consult", never "book"
budget_band "under_min"     -> verdict is "decline", polite referral template
missing is not empty        -> ask only for what is missing, one message, max 2 questions
existing_client is true     -> never send the first timer script
