The structure

Two GoHighLevel accounts under the plan Don already pays for. The Association is a 501(c)(4) and the Foundation is a 501(c)(3), so each keeps its own Stripe, bank and receipt wording. Money never mixes, and the app shows both in one place.

Association Stripe

  • Six yearly membership products: Supporting $25, Student $50, Advocate $125, Trustee $250, Guardian $500, Patron $2,500
  • Advocacy gifts, one time or monthly
  • Breakfast Club guest seats and sponsor tables

Foundation Stripe

  • Tax deductible gifts, one time, monthly or yearly
  • Gifts to a fund: Year End Appeal, Report Card research, Bill Wilson Award
  • Award Dinner seats and tables

Both take bank transfer and card. Donors can cover the fee. Failed and expiring payments trigger a polite email automatically.

The move

DonorView stays on until GoHighLevel has run one clean month.

Week 1
Export everything from DonorView. Set up both accounts, both Stripe accounts, fields, pipelines.
Week 2
Import contacts, donors, gifts, memberships and households. Match people who are in both. Check totals against DonorView.
Week 3
Receipts, forms, automations and the Constant Contact sync. Test every receipt for both organizations.
Week 4
The VCTA App goes live for the Executive Director and the board.
Weeks 5 and 6
Members and donors get their app login. Give and Join pages switch over on both websites.
About week 10
After one clean month, cancel DonorView.
