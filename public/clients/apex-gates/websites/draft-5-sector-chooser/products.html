Direct supplies and sales

Motors, tracks and parts, sold direct

Fence companies, builders and handy owners buy gate motors, tracks, remotes, safety beams and accessories from us direct. We supply nationally, and we can help you choose the right motor for the weight and size of your gate.

Our gate automation products are carefully evaluated and tested for quality, durability and economic performance, and many are sourced from local suppliers.

  • Gate motors and controllers
  • Gate tracks and rollers
  • Remotes, keypads and safety beams
  • Batteries and solar kits
