04 / FAQs

Good questions.

Do you offer maintenance contracts for commercial sites?

Yes. We offer tailored service and maintenance contracts so your automatic gates operate as they were designed to, and you are covered for insurance and OHS requirements.

Can you service a gate that someone else installed?

Yes. We service all brands of automatic gates, including Centsys, Ditec, BFT, FAAC, Nice, ATA, GDS and many others.

Can my existing gate be made automatic?

Most manually operated swing gates can be automated. A site visit, or emailed pictures with a plan layout of your gates and surrounds, is all we need to tell you if your gates are suitable.

Can I install a sliding gate on a sloping driveway?

Yes, but it is always best to rework the drive to suit a level track. A steep slope has limits and safety issues, for example when you open the gate by hand in a power cut. In that case we recommend an aluminium gate.

Do I need a power point on each side of my gate?

No. Even two piece gates only need power on one side. Some products need no power point at all, running on batteries charged by solar panels.
