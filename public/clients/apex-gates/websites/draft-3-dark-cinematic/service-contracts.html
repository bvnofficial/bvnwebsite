Questions

Service contract questions

Do you offer maintenance contracts for commercial sites?

Yes. We offer tailored service and maintenance contracts so your automatic gates operate as they were designed to, and you are covered for insurance and OHS requirements.

Can you service a gate that someone else installed?

Yes. We service all brands of automatic gates, including Centsys, Ditec, BFT, FAAC, Nice, ATA, GDS and many others.

What do you check on a service visit?

Motors, gearboxes, tracks, rollers, hinges, safety beams, sensors, remotes and batteries, depending on the gate. Anything worn goes in your report with a price to fix it.

How often should a commercial gate be serviced?

It depends on how often it opens and the conditions it works in. A busy gate on a coastal site needs more visits than a quiet one inland. We set the interval in your service plan.
