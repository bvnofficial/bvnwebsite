FAQs

Questions we get asked

Can't see yours? Call 0477 88 44 22 or email info@apexgates.com.au.

Do you offer maintenance contracts for commercial sites?

Yes. We offer tailored service and maintenance contracts so your automatic gates operate as they were designed to, and you are covered for insurance and OHS requirements.

Can you service a gate that someone else installed?

Yes. We service all brands of automatic gates, including Centsys, Ditec, BFT, FAAC, Nice, ATA, GDS and many others.

Can my existing gate be made automatic?

Most manually operated swing gates can be automated. A site visit, or emailed pictures with a plan layout of your gates and surrounds, is all we need to quickly tell you if your gates are suitable.

Will an automatic opener make my gate easier to open?

If your gate is hard to open because the hinges are seized or the wheel bearings have rusted, an opener will not fix that. Replacing those parts first should make the gate suitable for automation.

Can I install a sliding gate on a sloping driveway?

Yes. It is always best to rework the drive to suit a level track, because a steep slope has limits and safety issues, for example when you have to open the gate by hand in a power cut. In that case we recommend an aluminium gate.

Do I need a power point on each side of my gate?

No. Even two piece gates only need power on one side. Some of our products need no power point at all, running on batteries charged by solar panels, and most gate motors run on 12 or 24V DC.

What happens if there is a power cut?

Without battery backup you will need to release the gate by hand from inside the property. We can fit battery backup so your gate keeps working.
