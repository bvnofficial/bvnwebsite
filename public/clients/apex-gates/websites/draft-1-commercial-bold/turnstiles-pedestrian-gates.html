Turnstiles and pedestrian gates

Full height, bi directional and back to back turnstiles that control who walks on and off your site. Supplied, installed and connected to the access control you already use.

Request a quoteCall 0477 88 44 22

Overview

Control people the way you control vehicles

Vehicle gates only solve half of site access. Turnstiles make sure each person is checked in and out, one at a time. That matters on mine sites, depots and construction sites, and anywhere staff and visitors need to be counted.

Full height turnstiles stop people climbing over or slipping through behind someone else. Back to back units double the flow at shift change. Pedestrian gates give a wider path for deliveries, prams and wheelchairs beside the turnstiles.

Questions

Turnstiles and pedestrian gates: common questions

Do turnstiles work with our existing cards?

Usually, yes. Most turnstiles take a reader that matches the cards or fobs you already use.

What happens in an emergency?

Turnstiles can be set to release when the fire system triggers or the power fails, so people can leave freely. We set this up to suit your site's emergency plan.

Can you supply turnstiles without installing them?

Yes. We supply nationally, and we install across Perth, Rockingham and Mandurah.
