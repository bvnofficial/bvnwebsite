How apexgates.com.au scores today, and how the concepts score

We ran the same on-page checks on your current homepage and on every concept: titles, descriptions, headings, image text, business details for Google, sharing previews, mobile and lead capture.

These are on-page checks, the parts of SEO the website itself controls. Every concept is now a full 18 page website with its own page for each product, industry and service, and all 80 indexable pages pass every check. Ranking on Google also depends on a Google Business Profile with reviews and links from other sites. Those are the next steps once a design is chosen.

What we found on the current site

Beyond SEO: why commercial clients slip through

The commercial story is buried

Telstra, Western Power, the Water Corporation, mining, airports and seaports are only on the About page. The homepage reads residential.

Fixed: every concept names them in the first screen.

No way to ask for a quote

No contact or quote form on any page, only a phone number and email.

Fixed: a quote form with photo upload on every concept.

No service contract offer

Nothing tells a facility manager you offer maintenance contracts, the steadiest commercial work there is.

Fixed: a service contract section in all five.

Two numbers, no labels

0477 88 44 22 and 0409 865 250 appear with no hint which to call.

Fixed: labelled Office and Mobile, click to call.

Links that go to the wrong page

"Automated Boom Gates" opens the swing gates page, and several menu items are icons only.

Fixed: plain text menus that point where they say.

Typos and dated photos

"Safefy Bay" and "Turnstyles", and most images date from 2018 and 2019.

Fixed: corrected copy and your recent Instagram job photos.
