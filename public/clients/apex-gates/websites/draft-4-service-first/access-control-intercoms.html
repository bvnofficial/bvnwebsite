Overview

The brains behind every gate

A gate is only as secure as the system that decides when it opens. Access control links your gates, booms, bollards and turnstiles to the people allowed through them, using cards, fobs, PINs, remotes or phones.

Every entry is recorded, so you can see who came and went, and when. Add an intercom with audio and video and visitors can be checked and let in from the office or a phone. We work with brands including Paxton.

Questions

Access control and intercoms: common questions

Can you add access control to our existing gates?

Usually, yes. Most automatic gates can be connected to a reader, keypad or intercom.

What brands do you use?

We supply and install a range of systems, including Paxton, and match the system to your site.

Can we see who opened the gate?

Yes. Access control systems keep an audit trail of every entry and exit.
