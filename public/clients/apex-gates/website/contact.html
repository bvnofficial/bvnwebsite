What happens next

Questions

Quote questions

What do you need from me for a quote?

The site address, what you want to automate or replace, and a few photos of the gate and its surrounds. A rough plan with the opening width helps us quote faster.

Do you sell parts and motors direct?

Yes. We offer direct supplies and sales of gate motors, tracks, remotes and accessories, and we supply nationally.

My gate has stopped. What should I do?

Call the office on 0477 88 44 22. Tell us the brand of motor if you can see it, and what the gate is doing.

Which areas do you cover?

Perth metro, Rockingham, Mandurah and the Peel, from our Malaga head office. We also work outside the metro area, and we supply nationally.

Do you work on homes as well as commercial sites?

Yes. We automate, install and service gates for homes, strata complexes and commercial sites.
