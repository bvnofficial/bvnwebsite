FAQs

Questions we get asked

Can't see yours? Call 0477 88 44 22 or email info@apexgates.com.au.

Do you work on homes as well as commercial sites?

Yes. We automate, install and service gates for homes, strata complexes and commercial sites.

Which areas do you cover?

Perth metro, Rockingham, Mandurah and the Peel, from our Malaga head office. We also work outside the metro area, and we supply nationally.

Can my existing gate be made automatic?

Most manually operated swing gates can be automated. A site visit, or emailed photos with a plan layout of your gates and surrounds, is all we need to quickly tell you if your gates are suitable.

Do I need a power point on each side of my gate?

No. Even two piece gates only need power on one side. Some of our products need no power point at all, running on batteries charged by solar panels, and most gate motors run on 12 or 24V DC.

What happens if there is a power cut?

Without battery backup you will need to release the gate by hand from inside the property. We can fit battery backup so your gate keeps working.

Can you service a gate that someone else installed?

Yes. We service all brands of automatic gates, including Centsys, Ditec, BFT, FAAC, Nice, ATA, GDS and many others.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer tailored service and maintenance contracts for commercial sites and strata complexes, so your automatic gates operate as they were designed to and you are covered for insurance and OHS requirements.
