01

Car Finance Fit Check

A shopper pastes the link to the car they want, or picks make, model and year. Five quick questions later they see an estimated weekly repayment range for that exact car, what they'll need to bring, and a button to book a Financial Fit Session or text Ben. Every check lands in GHL as a tagged, scored lead. Estimate only, not a credit assessment.

Fixes: landing page leads who never answer the phone. They've already told you the car, the amount and their job.

For your customers

4 apps
02

Text Ben, any hour

The AI agent on Messenger and SMS, trained on your real past chats. It answers in seconds, asks your qualifying questions, says no politely to anyone under your minimum and books the Fit Session into Ben's calendar. Try it live on the call.

Fixes: leads that go cold overnight, chats lost inside Messenger
03

Document drop

Applicants snap their licence, payslips and bank statements on their phone. A checklist shows what's missing; reminders go out until it's done, then the file is ready for Zinc.

Fixes: applications stalled for days waiting on payslips
04

My Loan tracker

A link the customer keeps: application sent, with the lender, approved, settlement booked, keys. Like tracking a parcel, for their car loan.

Fixes: "any news?" calls during the busiest part of the day
05

Keys day

On settlement the customer gets a congratulations message and a link to record a 20 second video with their new car. Videos feed straight into your ads and nurture texts.

Fixes: no testimonials or client videos for the nurture content

For Ben, Luisa and Leo

3 apps
06

Speed to Lead phone app

A new lead buzzes Ben's phone with the car, amount and job. One tap calls. If nobody calls in 60 seconds it passes to Leo. After the call, one tap logs the outcome and the cadence moves on its own.

Fixes: the 1 minute rule living in a coaching note instead of a phone
07

Revival dialer

Works the 1,200 old leads newest first in batches of 100: the opener on screen, double call, one tap text, and anyone who says "not yet" goes to nurture with their email captured.

Fixes: a year of paid leads sitting in a spreadsheet
08

Wording checker

Paste any ad, text or script. It flags "pre approval", promises of approval, missing opt outs and risky claims before anything goes out.

Fixes: ASIC wording risk in fast moving ads and texts

For growing the direct channel

4 apps
09

Referral partner app

Mechanics, car yards, accountants and past customers send a lead in 30 seconds from their phone and watch it move to settled. HQ ranks partners by settled deals.

Fixes: referrer enquiries arriving by email and never tracked
10

Ad money board

Cost per lead, per Fit Session and per settled deal for every ad, Messenger vs landing page vs Google, updated daily.

Fixes: guessing which ads actually make money
11

Direct vs dealership tracker

The 50% direct milestone as a live number, month by month, with a target line.

Fixes: the revenue mix only known at month end
12

Car match alerts

Leads in nurture tell you what they want. When their budget and timing line up, they get a friendly "still after a Hilux?" text and land back on Ben's call list.

Fixes: "not now" leads that never come back

Making every deal count

3 apps
13

Settlements sent back to Meta

Every Fit Session and settled deal goes back to Meta with its value, matched to the ad that found it, so Meta hunts for people who actually settle.

Fixes: ads that chase form fillers who never answer the phone
14

Repeat business engine
