Frequently asked questions

For most vehicles, QLD law requires front side windows to allow at least 35% VLT (visible light transmission), while rear side and back windows can be darker. Our installers fit every job to the current legal standard, so you're always road-legal.

A typical full-car fitting takes around 2–4 hours depending on the vehicle. We'll give you a clear time when you book, and same-day appointments are often available.

Yes. Our premium films come with a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, cracking and discolouration, backed by over 25 years of TintGard workmanship.

For many home, office and commercial jobs we come to you across Brisbane, Ipswich, Logan, the Gold Coast and surrounds. Let us know your location in the booking form and we'll confirm availability.

We recommend leaving windows up for 3–5 days while the film cures. A little haze or small water pockets during curing are normal and clear on their own.

We service Brisbane City, Ipswich, Logan, Gold Coast, Moreton Bay, Redland City and the Scenic Rim. Our workshop is in Augustine Heights — drop in or book a mobile visit.
