Built in to every workflow

  • Every workflow stops the moment the client books, so nobody gets an offer after they've already paid.
  • Wrinkle relaxers, fillers and bio stimulating injections never get a price, a discount or a brand name. They get a complimentary consult with Nurse Leo or Nurse Maria instead.
  • Package holders never get a discount. They get a reminder to book from their package.
  • Marketing emails carry an unsubscribe link and SMS carry Reply STOP. A "do not market" tag stops everything for a client.
  • A worried reply pauses all automations for that client until a nurse clears it.
