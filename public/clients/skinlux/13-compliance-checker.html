What it checks

  • Injectable brand names such as Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Juvederm and Restylane (TGA)
  • Prices or dollar amounts near injectable words
  • Discounts, limited time wording, spots or freebies near injectables (AHPRA cosmetic guidelines 2023)
  • Client quotes, reviews or testimonials near injectables
  • Before and after claims
  • Guaranteed, permanent or risk free results
  • Pain free or painless claims
  • Superlatives such as best, leading or number one in health claims
  • Missing unsubscribe on emails and missing Reply STOP on SMS (Spam Act 2003)
  • Messages that don't say they're from Skinlux

A helper, not legal advice. Final check by Jellie or a nurse.
