Staff app

One phone app for the team: today's clients, the shared call back list, client replies that need a human, and your own week at a glance.

Demo with sample data. In the live version this connects to Timely and GoHighLevel.

How the team uses it

1Sign in

Each therapist taps their name and clinic, then a 4 digit PIN. Their day loads straight from Timely.

2Today

Every client booked with you, with the things you need to know before they sit down: first visit, course progress, and anything flagged on their intake form.

3Treatment done

Timely doesn't share when a treatment is finished. This one tap is that signal: it sends the right aftercare, books a check in message, suggests the next visit from the treatment interval and logs any product sold.

4Call backs

Your therapists already answer the phones, so the follow up list is shared. Abandoned online bookings and clients who did not rebook land here. Anything waiting too long turns red, and every call or SMS is logged so nobody rings the same client twice.

5Inbox

Automatic check in messages go out after treatments. Most replies are "all good" and close themselves. Anything that mentions redness, pain or worry is flagged here for a person to answer, with quick replies written in your words.

6Me

Each therapist sees their own week: clients seen, how many rebooked, and products sold. It feeds the scorecard and bonus tracking.

Compliance built in

Messages about injectable treatments never include prices, offers or product brand names. The app removes them before anything is sent to a client.
