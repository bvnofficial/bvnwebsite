HQ

Keighran HQ

One screen for Cindy and Duane: new enquiries with the conflict check, every matter from enquiry to final invoice, key dates across all files, referrers due a call, client feedback, team workload, onboarding, overdue invoices and whether the website is up.

Fixes: checking inboxes, spreadsheets and the matter system to know how the week is going
01

Key Date Finder

Drop in a lease, a contract for sale or a hotel management agreement. In seconds it lists every date that matters: sunset date, settlement, option windows, rent reviews, notice periods, make good. One tap loads them into Key Dates, with reminders to the lawyer and the client.

Fixes: dates read by hand from 80 page documents, the missed option window nobody wants to explain

For your clients

4 apps
02

Deal Tracker client app

Every client sees their matter like a delivery tracker: what's done, what's next, who is waiting on whom, the settlement countdown and a secure place to upload documents. Weekly plain English updates arrive on their own.

Fixes: "any update?" emails and calls, clients feeling in the dark
03

Smart intake and conflict check

A short enquiry form that asks the right questions for property, hotel or construction work, runs the conflict check against your client list, then sends the engagement letter for online signing.

Fixes: enquiries sitting in info@, engagement letters chased by hand
04

After hours receptionist

An AI voice and chat receptionist that answers when the office is closed, takes the matter details, checks urgency and books a call with the right lawyer. Every call lands in HQ with a summary.

Fixes: developers and hotel operators who call at 7pm and try the next firm
05

Feedback and reviews

When a matter closes the client gets a two question survey. Happy clients get a Google review link; anyone under four stars goes to Duane for a call first.

Fixes: great results that never turn into public reviews

For your team

4 apps
06

Pathways hub

The cadet and graduate program in one app: onboarding checklist, mentor check ins, rotations, study support and progress for every Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cadet and graduate. A public page tells their story to clients and future cadets.

Fixes: the firm's mission living in people's heads instead of a system
07

Client update writer

The lawyer types three rough lines; it drafts a clear weekly update in the firm's voice for them to check and send.

Fixes: updates skipped on busy weeks
08

Precedents and know how

Search your own precedents, checklists and past advice by question, not by folder name.

Fixes: rebuilding a clause that was perfect two matters ago
09

Event card scanner

Photograph a business card at a Chamber or Supply Nation event. The contact lands in HQ tagged to the event, a thank you email goes out and a follow up reminder is set.

Fixes: a stack of cards in a drawer after every event

For Cindy and Duane

4 apps
10

Referrer portal

Accountants, advisers and past colleagues send a matter in 30 seconds from their phone and see how it's going. HQ shows who sends the most work and who is due a coffee.

Fixes: referrers never hearing back, no view of where work comes from
11

Firm operations board

Overdue invoices with polite reminders, insurance and certificate renewals, leave, subscriptions and new starter tasks, all with owners and due dates.

Fixes: renewals found the week they lapse
12

Fundraising pages

A page per cause (Coastrek and the rest) with a team leaderboard, progress bar and thank you messages to every donor.

Fixes: fundraising run from spreadsheets and group texts
13

Website watch

Checks the site every five minutes and tests the contact form every morning. If either breaks, Cindy gets a text within minutes.

Fixes: a site or form that's down for days before anyone notices

Tender and panel tracker
