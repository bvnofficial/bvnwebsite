From enquiry to signed costs agreement

Today a colleague tracks every enquiry by hand: send the capability statement, check for a reply, follow up, send the costs agreement, follow up again. This is how GoHighLevel will run those steps for you, and what stays in your hands.

The pipeline

Every enquiry sits on one board in GoHighLevel, in the stage it has reached. Nobody has to remember who is waiting on what.

1

New enquiry

Logged with who referred it and what it's about.

2

3

Following up

4

Costs agreement requested

5

A link they open, review and sign.

6

Following up

Reminders until it's signed.

7

Engaged

8

Not proceeding

The automations

Six workflows run the stages above. Every email is written with you and nothing goes out until you approve the wording.

1

New enquiry comes in

Starts when an enquiry is logged
  • Most of your work arrives by email or a call to Duane, so there are three ways in: forward the email to a firm address in GoHighLevel, fill a 30 second enquiry form on the phone app after a call, or a contact form on a service landing page.
  • The contact is created once. If the same person contacts you again, it joins the same record with the full history.
  • The right person gets a notification and a task: send the capability statement.
2

Capability statement goes out

Starts when you press send on the enquiry
  • A capability statement template built from the way you already tailor them: the client's name, the type of deal, and only the services and team members that fit.
  • Sent from your firm address as a branded document, logged against the contact, and the enquiry moves to stage 2 on its own.
3

No reply follow up

Starts if there's no reply to the capability statement
  • A short, personal follow up email after 2 days, and a second one after 5 days.
  • On day 10, a task to call instead of another email.
  • The moment they reply, the follow ups stop. If someone replies to Duane directly, one click marks it as replied.
Day 2: emailDay 5: emailDay 10: call task
4

Costs agreement sent as a link

Starts when the client asks for a costs agreement
  • Your costs agreement as a template with itemised line items you pick for each matter: fixed fees, hourly items, disbursements.
  • The client opens a link, reads it in their browser and signs online. You see when it was opened.
5

Costs agreement follow up

Starts if the agreement isn't signed
  • A reminder after 2 days and another after 5 days, then a task for a personal call.
  • When it's signed: the enquiry moves to Engaged, Cindy and Duane are notified, and a task is created to open the matter in LEAP or Clio.
Day 2: reminderDay 5: reminderDay 8: call task
6

Stay in touch

For enquiries that go quiet or aren't ready yet
  • An occasional, personal check in so the firm's name is front of mind when they or someone they know needs a property, hotel or construction lawyer.
  • Optional, and only with wording you approve.

What you'll see each day

In GoHighLevel and Keighran HQ

  • New enquiries and who referred them
  • Capability statements and costs agreements waiting on a reply
  • Calls due today
  • Enquiries won and lost this month, and why
  • Where your work comes from: Duane's network, referrers, landing pages

Your email and client data

  • GoHighLevel never gets access to anyone's inbox. Emails send from one firm address through your domain, set up with standard DNS records.
  • Each person has their own login, and you control who sees what.
  • Your matters stay in LEAP or Clio. GoHighLevel only holds the enquiry until it's engaged.
  • Nothing is sent or published without your approval.

What I need from you

  • Two or three capability statements you've sent to clients, so the template matches how you tailor them
  • Your costs agreement template and the usual line items
  • The address to send from, for example info@keighranlegal.com
  • Access to your domain's DNS, or the name of whoever manages your website or IT
  • Who gets notified for new enquiries, and who handles follow up calls
  • Your logo and email signature
