The Keighran Pathways program

Keighran Legal + Advisory was founded to open pathways for young Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander lawyers into corporate law. This page tells that story in the cadets' own words (with their OK), shows the program's real numbers, and gives future cadets one place to apply. It's also the page you attach to tenders and capability statements.
