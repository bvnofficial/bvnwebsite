      Tag check

      A QR code on every anchor tag. A gutter cleaner or roofer scans it before clipping on and sees if that anchor is safe to use today.

      Each scan is logged, so you can see which buildings get used most and who is working on your clients' roofs. An out of date or failed anchor shows a clear stop screen and a button to call the office.
