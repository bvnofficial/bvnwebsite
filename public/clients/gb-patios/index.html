    Crew Board

    Every won job on one screen: which crew, which day, whether materials and approval are in. Crews check it on their phones. Drag a job to another day (or tap the job, then tap a day) and the customer gets a text with the new date.

    If you already run jobs in ServiceM8, Tradify or simPRO, this board becomes a link between that tool and GoHighLevel instead.
