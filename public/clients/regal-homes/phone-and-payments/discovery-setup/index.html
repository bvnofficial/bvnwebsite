Phone & Payments System — Discovery & Setup
Milestone 1 Deliverable · Discovery & Setup
Prepared by Benjamin Yson, BVN July 2026 Prepared for Don Brodt, Regal Care Homes

This document confirms the completed discovery and setup for the combined telephone and ACH payment system. It records the agreed architecture, the account and ownership structure, the number and call flow design, the payment lifecycle design, the equipment plan, and the setup steps. It represents the work for the first milestone and is the reference we build against through the pilot and rollout.

1. Confirmed system architecture

Two systems, both owned by Regal and both connected to the existing CRM, working as one.

Telephone

Every home number and one new primary Regal Homes number are held in a Regal owned Twilio account. Calls route to a single AI receptionist that screens solicitations, takes messages, directs vendors to email, and captures genuine inquiries into the CRM. Real calls transfer to the home, and each home has its own routing and escalation rules.

ACH Payments

Resident payments run through CSG Forte, with one merchant account per legal entity settling into that entity's own bank. Families enroll through Forte's secure hosted page. Payment status flows into the CRM through an automation layer, while all bank details stay inside Forte and never enter the CRM.

Because the CRM was already built by BVN, both systems plug into the existing pipelines, tags, fields, and calendars rather than sitting beside them.

3. Telephone design

Number plan

  • One primary Regal Homes number becomes the public facing number for the website, advertising, and referrals
  • The existing home numbers are ported into the Regal owned Twilio account and kept for current families, physicians, hospice, vendors, and printed materials
  • The primary number answers as Regal Homes, with the AI identifying the home or purpose; each home number answers with that home's name

Call flow and escalation, per home

  • Screen: solicitations and robocalls filtered out before any person is involved
  • Route: vendors and care providers have a message taken and are pointed to email for routine notices; simple questions become a task in the CRM for the right person
  • Capture: genuine prospective resident inquiries are engaged, logged into the Family Inquiry pipeline, and either booked or handed straight to a person
  • Escalate: urgent or person specific calls transfer live to a designated line; if no one answers, a priority message and instant text go out. After hours, urgent calls escalate and the rest arrive as a morning summary

Equipment at each home

A dedicated house cellphone is the recommended primary device. It stays at the home, works during an internet outage, dials 911 natively and reliably, and helps with telehealth. A softphone app on it presents the correct home caller ID for outbound business calls. 911 always goes through the phone's native dialer, and each Twilio number's emergency address is registered as a secondary safeguard.

4. ACH payment design

Entities, banks, and settlement

  • Two Forte merchant accounts, one per legal entity, each settling into that entity's own bank, currently Bank of America
  • The settlement bank is a setting per entity, so banks can change, an entity can be added or replaced, and a home can move between entities, all without rebuilding the integration
  • All authorization and transaction records export cleanly, so a future move of bank or processor is a configuration and export task, not a rebuild

Payment lifecycle handled

  • Secure hosted family enrollment and ACH authorization, stored in Forte
  • Recurring monthly drafts, initial deposits, one time charges, and prorated amounts
  • Rate increases and amount changes, with families notified
  • Credits and refunds, suspended or terminated drafts with authorizations retained
  • Failed payments and ACH returns captured with their codes, retry rules, and family notifications
  • Reconciliation by resident, home, and entity, and export of payment and authorization records

Payment status in the CRM

No money or bank data is native to the CRM. Status appears through a custom field, Payment Status, with the values paid, pending, failed, returned, refunded, and canceled, supported by status tags and by workflows that trigger reminders or staff alerts on a failed or returned payment. Family bank information remains entirely inside Forte.

5. The pilot

We begin with a reversible pilot at one home while Spectrum stays active. We forward that home's number to the AI, configure its greeting, call flow, and escalation, and test transfers, messages, family inquiries, after hours calls, and rollback. We expand to the remaining numbers only once the pilot performs well, so there is a safe fallback at every step.
