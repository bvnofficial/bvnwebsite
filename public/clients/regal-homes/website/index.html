Welcome to Regal Care

Personal care, in a real neighborhood home — not an institution.

Tucked into Simi Valley's quiet Texas Tract neighborhood, just east of Tapo Street, Regal Care offers the warmth of a family home with the dependability of professional, around-the-clock care. With only six residents, every person receives attention that larger facilities simply can't match.

  • Six beds, by design. A small home means familiar faces, consistent caregivers, and genuine relationships.
  • Awake staff, day and night. Someone is always up and ready — including throughout the night.
  • A garden to enjoy. A beautifully maintained backyard with a vegetable garden, covered patio, and BBQ.
