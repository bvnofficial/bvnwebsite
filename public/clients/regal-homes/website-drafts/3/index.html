The Regal app

Stay close to your loved one from anywhere. The Regal app brings families and our office together in one place. Regal sets up your account, so you sign in with the email the home has on file. You can also log in from any web browser, right here on our website.

  • Message the office and send a photo or a document anytime
  • Book a visit, sign in when you arrive, and let the house know when you are picking your loved one up
  • See your monthly invoice and pay by bank
  • Sign admission papers and state forms on your phone
  • Get news and events from the home, and watch our welcome videos

About Us

We’re Don and Ribka Brodt, husband-and-wife founders of Regal. We live and work in Ventura County, where we’re also raising our daughter. Having lived and traveled around the world, we’ve come to deeply appreciate the beauty and tight-knit spirit of this region. There’s no better place to call home—and no better community to serve. We bring different strengths to the table, but we share one driving belief: every senior deserves to feel safe, valued, and at home.
