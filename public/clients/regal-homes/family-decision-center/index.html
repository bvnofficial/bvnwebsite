Common questions

Answers, in plain language.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on the home, the room, and the level of care needed. Our homes are private pay. Regal Care and Regal Residence start at $6,500 a month, and The Orchard starts at $9,500. We are glad to talk through what would apply to your family's situation.

What levels of care do you provide?+

We care for a broad range of needs, from residents who simply want the ease of not managing a household, to those who need extensive support. We are not licensed for 24-hour skilled nursing, and a short assessment confirms we can meet your loved one's needs safely.

Do you accept residents with dementia?+

Yes. Residents with a dementia diagnosis can be accepted and cared for with us, confirmed at assessment.

Can someone stay if they go on hospice?+

Yes. Every room in all three homes is approved for hospice care, and our goal is to help residents age in place whenever it is safe and legally possible, so moves can be minimized.

Who is there overnight?+

Every one of our homes has awake staff overnight, every night. We do not rely on staff sleeping on shift.

What are meals like?+

Real, home-cooked meals served together at the table, three a day plus snacks, with special or prescribed diets accommodated.

How do you handle medications?+

All medications and any changes require a written order from the physician. We cannot make a change based on a phone call alone, but we are glad to coordinate with your doctor's office.

What are visiting hours?+

There are no set visiting hours, you are welcome anytime. The one thing we ask is that mealtimes are our busiest window, so during that time we ask visitors not to interrupt the caregivers' routine.

Do you take insurance, Medi-Cal, or VA benefits?+

Regal is private pay, which means families pay us directly. Some families receive reimbursement or assistance through programs such as long-term-care insurance or veterans benefits, handled directly between the family and the program. We do not bill Medi-Cal directly, and a member of our team can explain how our billing works.

Moving in

How it works, step by step.

We keep it simple, and we handle the parts we can so your family does not have to.

1
A conversation
We talk through your loved one's needs, timing, and which home might fit.
2
A visit
Come see the home and meet the team, whenever works for your family.
3
A quick assessment
We confirm we can meet the care needs safely. We take care of our own forms.
4
Physician's report
Your loved one's own doctor completes a Physician's Report and TB clearance, valid up to a year.
5
Move-in day
A guided, gentle arrival, with medication and room setup handled for you.
6
The first weeks
We stay close during settling in, and keep you updated as your loved one adjusts.
